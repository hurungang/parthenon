## How acceptance works

When you open or update a pull request, the CLA workflow checks whether your
GitHub account has already signed this agreement.

If not, the bot asks you to reply on the pull request with this exact text:

`I have read the Parthenon Contributor License Agreement and I hereby accept its terms.`

That comment records your acceptance and allows the CLA status check to pass.
